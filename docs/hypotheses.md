# Falsifiable Scientific Hypotheses & Isolation Matrix

This document defines the primary scientific hypotheses governing the SeismoFNO research program, designed to isolate competing failure mechanisms under strict Popperian falsification criteria.

---

## 1. Primary Scientific Hypotheses

### Hypothesis 1: The Spectral Acausality Hypothesis (Mechanism A)
> **Statement:** *The systematic degradation of standard FNO in the post-yield regime ($\sim 57.5\%$ error vs. $10.8\%$ elastic error) is primarily caused by acausal temporal mixing in the global Fourier transform. Replacing global spectral convolutions with a strictly causal temporal operator (Causal TCN or Structured State-Space Model) will reduce post-yield displacement error by at least $20\%$ absolute (from $57.5\% \to < 37.5\%$) on identical held-out earthquake records.*

- **Independent Variable:** Causality of the temporal receptive field (Acausal Global FFT vs. Strictly Left-Padded Causal Convolutions/SSM).
- **Controlled Variables:** Parameter budget ($\sim 1.2\text{M}$ params), input channels, training data split, optimizer, learning rate schedule, target variables ($u, F_R, E_h$).
- **Expected Outcome:** Causal models maintain phase alignment during cyclic degradation, reducing post-yield error.
- **Falsification Criterion:** If a strictly causal model (Causal TCN or S4) achieves equal or worse post-yield error ($\ge 55\%$) compared to standard FNO, Hypothesis 1 is **FALSIFIED** (proving acausality is not the primary bottleneck).

---

### Hypothesis 2: The Explicit State Representation Hypothesis (Mechanism B)
> **Statement:** *Even with causal convolutions, models that do not maintain an explicit internal state variable $z(t)$ struggle to track permanent plastic offset $u_p = \int \dot{u}_p d\tau$. Adding an auxiliary state channel or an explicit recurrent state-space variable will reduce residual drift error ($|u_{\text{residual}} - \hat{u}_{\text{residual}}|$) by $> 30\%$ relative to state-free causal models.*

- **Independent Variable:** Internal state representation (State-free Causal vs. Explicit State-Space / Recurrent State).
- **Controlled Variables:** Model depth, receptive field length, training loss.
- **Expected Outcome:** State-aware architectures accurately track the shifted elastic origin during multiple hysteretic load reversals.
- **Falsification Criterion:** If adding explicit internal state tracking does not reduce residual plastic drift error by at least $15\%$, Hypothesis 2 is **FALSIFIED**.

---

### Hypothesis 3: The Spectral Truncation & High-Frequency Loss Hypothesis (Mechanism C)
> **Statement:** *The post-yield failure of FNO is not solely temporal acausality, but spectral truncation: cutting off modes at $K=128$ filters out high-frequency harmonics generated by the non-smooth slope discontinuity at yielding ($k_0 \to \alpha k_0$). Increasing mode count $K$ from $128 \to 512$ will resolve the sharp yield transition in standard FNO without changing causality.*

- **Independent Variable:** Fourier mode cutoff $K \in [16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512]$.
- **Controlled Variables:** Network width, depth, dataset.
- **Expected Outcome:** Higher $K$ improves high-frequency resolution but fails to resolve phase lag if acausality dominates.
- **Falsification Criterion:** If increasing $K$ from $128 \to 512$ reduces post-yield error to $< 25\%$, then Hypothesis 1 (Acausality) is **FALSIFIED**, and spectral truncation is confirmed as the primary bottleneck.

---

### Hypothesis 4: The Structural Conditioning Modulation Hypothesis (Mechanism D)
> **Statement:** *Static structural parameter channels ($T_n, \zeta, u_y, \alpha$) are insufficient to modulate global spectral weights across varying structural natural frequencies. Applying Feature-wise Linear Modulation (FiLM) or dynamic hypernetworks to modulate spectral weights directly will reduce structural parameter interpolation error by $> 25\%$.*

- **Independent Variable:** Parameter conditioning mechanism (Channel concatenation vs. FiLM / Dynamic Weight Modulation).
- **Controlled Variables:** Base operator architecture, training split.
- **Falsification Criterion:** If FiLM conditioning does not statistically improve parameter generalization across held-out structural periods, Hypothesis 4 is **FALSIFIED**.

---

### Hypothesis 5: The Thermodynamic Energy Consistency Hypothesis (Mechanism E)
> **Statement:** *Supervising the surrogate with a scale-regularized energy consistency loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{energy}} = \|\hat{E}_h - \int \hat{F}_R d\hat{u}\| / \max(\|\hat{E}_h\|, E_i, 1.0)$ acts as a thermodynamic regularizer that prevents non-physical trajectory drift and improves restoring force $F_R(t)$ prediction by $> 15\%$ across all ductility levels.*

- **Independent Variable:** Inclusion of regularized energy consistency loss ($\lambda_{\text{energy}} = 0.0$ vs. $\lambda_{\text{energy}} = 0.10$).
- **Controlled Variables:** Data loss weight, architecture, test dataset.
- **Falsification Criterion:** If removing $\mathcal{L}_{\text{energy}}$ results in $\le 2.0\%$ difference in restoring force and energy metrics, Hypothesis 5 is **FALSIFIED** (proving physics loss is ineffective).
